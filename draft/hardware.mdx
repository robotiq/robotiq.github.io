# Sense

As humans, we rely on our sense of touch to manipulate objects with precision — feeling resistance, detecting slip, and adjusting grip force instinctively. Robots can approach that same capability using force/torque sensors and tactile sensor fingertips.

---

## Force Torque Sensor — FT300-S

<div style={{textAlign: 'center', margin: '2rem 0'}}>
  <img
    src="/img/FT-300-S_3Drender_white-background.png"

The FT300-S is a compact force/torque sensor mounted between the robot flange and the end-effector. It measures forces and torques on all six axes, giving your robot real-time awareness of how it physically interacts with its environment.

<div style={{textAlign: 'center', margin: '1.5rem 0'}}>
  <img
    src="/img/Force torque sensor marking.png"
    alt="FT300-S — six-axis force and torque measurement"
    style={{maxHeight: '220px', objectFit: 'contain'}}
  />
  <div style={{fontSize: '0.8rem', color: '#979C9E', marginTop: '0.5rem'}}>
    The FT300-S measures forces (Fx, Fy, Fz) and torques (Tx, Ty, Tz) on all six axes.
  </div>
</div>

**Typical applications:**
- Apply a controlled force against a surface — polishing, deburring, surface following
- Insert a peg into a hole using force-guided search
- Detect unexpected collisions and trigger a safe stop

## Tactile Sensor Fingertips — TSF85

  <img src="/img/TSF85 tactile finger 1.png" alt="TSF85 Tactile Fingertip — front view"  style={{maxHeight: '200px', objectFit: 'contain'}} />
  <img src="/img/TSF85 tactile finger 2.png" alt="TSF85 Tactile Fingertip — side view"   style={{maxHeight: '200px', objectFit: 'contain'}} />
  <img src="/img/TSF85 tactile finger 3.png" alt="TSF85 Tactile Fingertip — sensor grid" style={{maxHeight: '200px', objectFit: 'contain'}} />
</div>

The TSF85 are tactile sensor fingertips that replace the standard fingers on the 2F-85 gripper. Each fingertip embeds a grid of pressure sensors that map the contact distribution across the grasped surface in real time.

**Typical applications:**
- Identify objects by their contact shape and pressure signature
- Detect slip before a part is dropped and react instantly
- Evaluate grip quality and adapt closing force on the fly

For full specifications, visit the [TSF85 product page](https://robotiq.com/tactile-sensor-fingertips).
