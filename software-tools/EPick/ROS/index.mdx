The EPick vacuum gripper is controlled by writing its registers using the
Modbus RTU protocol. You will find detailed information in the EPick
manual available on the Robotiq support page: https://robotiq.com/support

While not developed and supported by Robotiq, a community ROS driver project
is available:

- **Reference:** [PickNikRobotics/ros2_epick_gripper](https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/ros2_epick_gripper)
