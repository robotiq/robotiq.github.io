Robotiq is currently working to provide an officially supported ROS 2 driver
for the 2F-85, 2F-140, and Hand-E grippers.

Grippers are controlled by writing their registers using the Modbus RTU
protocol. You will find detailed information in the gripper manual available
on the Robotiq support page: https://robotiq.com/support

While not developed and supported by Robotiq, a community ROS driver project
targets this distro:

- **Reference:** [PickNikRobotics/ros2_robotiq_gripper](https://github.com/PickNikRobotics/ros2_robotiq_gripper)
